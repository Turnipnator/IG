# Research Protocol

Follow this protocol when undertaking any research, strategy analysis, or market investigation task.

---

## 1. Decompose First
Before doing anything, break the question into specific sub-questions.
Do not start gathering data until you have a clear list of what you are looking for.

## 2. Form Competing Hypotheses
Generate at least two or three competing explanations or approaches before committing to one.
Do not anchor on the first pattern or result you find.

## 3. Track Confidence
Label every finding with a confidence level:
- **HIGH** — multiple corroborating sources or clear evidence
- **MEDIUM** — some evidence but gaps remain
- **LOW** — speculative or based on limited data

## 4. Self-Critique Each Step
After each step ask:
- What would disprove this finding?
- Am I missing a simpler explanation?
- Have I checked for survivorship bias or data snooping?

## 5. Persist Findings
Update a `research_notes.md` file as you go so findings are not lost between sessions.
Structure it as: Question → Hypotheses → Evidence → Confidence → Next Steps.

## 6. Surface Uncertainty
Never mask uncertainty with confident prose.
If you do not know something, say so and flag it as a gap to investigate.

## 7. Conclude with a Summary
End every research task with:
- The most supported hypothesis and why
- What was ruled out and why
- Remaining open questions
- Suggested next actions
